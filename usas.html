Internet access technologies

Internet access can be delivered to homes and devices through several technologies. Availability and performance depend on local infrastructure, location, network capacity, and other conditions.

⌁

Fiber optic

Uses pulses of light transmitted through optical fibers. It can provide high capacity and consistent performance where fiber infrastructure is available.

▤

Cable internet

Uses cable networks to transmit data. Performance depends on the network technology, available capacity, and service conditions.

◉

Fixed wireless

Delivers data through radio signals between network equipment and a receiving device. Distance, obstacles, and weather conditions may affect coverage.

▥

Mobile internet

Uses cellular networks to connect phones, tablets, and compatible devices. The experience varies with coverage, network congestion, and device capabilities.

Customer support

Identify the provider's official support channels and the procedures available for questions or service issues.

Basic connectivity concepts

What does Mbps mean?

Mbps means megabits per second. It is a unit used to describe data transfer speed on an internet connection.

What is latency?

Latency is the time it takes for data to travel from a device to a destination and receive a response. Lower latency is often useful for interactive activities such as video calls and online gaming.

What can affect Wi-Fi?

Distance from the router, walls, interference, router placement, and device capabilities can all affect wireless signal quality.
